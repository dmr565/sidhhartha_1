About us

At Sai Siddhartha High School, we work towards bringing about the holistic development of the students in a stress-free environment to enhance intellectual, social, and cultural vivacity among the learners. The school aims to impart broad-based education so as to enable the students to become worthy and dynamic leaders of tomorrow.

The school is child-centered and provides a child friendly ambience. Sai Siddhartha High School takes great pride in its excellent infrastructure, which has been suitably planned to accommodate learning and all round development of the child. The school provides a range of facilities to keep the child happy and feel at home as they learn and grow mentally, physically and socially.

Curriculum

At Sai Siddhartha High School, Campus spaces are architected to suit children of designated age-groups and seniority. We have imagined the infrastructure with an aim to uphold and promote the sanctity of learning together with the fun of growing up, both contributing for a higher purpose.

We have designed novel initiatives that keep children engaged with a new form of learning, we have also interlaced technology and infrastructure to act as a key enabler in the learning process. Students are encouraged to proactively share knowledge through the projects.

Our faculty members at Sai Siddhartha High School are not only highly experienced professionals but also deeply committed to creating a nurturing environment that promotes learning and personal growth.

By fostering a supportive and engaging atmosphere, our faculty members encourage students to explore their potential and excel in their educational journey. They are committed to providing students with the tools and knowledge they need to succeed academically and personally.

Imparting knowledge is considered one of the noblest activities, shaping minds and futures. Teachers at Sai Siddhartha High School embody this ethos, employing innovative teaching methodologies that foster the development of global citizens.

We firmly believe that cultivating good values, fostering an appreciation for life, and encouraging curiosity are fundamental to a child's upbringing. By instilling higher standards and moral values, we equip children with the confidence to navigate life's challenges with resilience and integrity.

The ICSE envisions a robust, vibrant and holistic school education that will engender excellence in every sphere of human endeavor. The Board is committed to provide quality education to pro-mote intellectual, social and cultural vivacity among its learners.

National Education Policy 2020 Education Policy lays particular emphasis on the development of the creative potential of each individual.

to reflect, respect, aspire, explore and work for more.
