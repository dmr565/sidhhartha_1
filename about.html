About

Sai Siddhartha High School

Sai Siddhartha high school is a dream destination for students who wish to have holistic education, excellent blending of curricular and co-curricular programs. Sai Siddhartha high school is an institution that is envisaged to provide young students with a tradition of excellence providing a healthy environment. Our goal is to provide value-driven education based on the best of the National Curriculum in a safe and conducive environment. We inculcate creativity in all our teaching and learning methods by providing exciting opportunities and experiences both in and beyond the classroom.

We strive to provide holistic growth along with the efficiency and vibrancy of an experienced teaching staff. The school is child-centered and provides a child-friendly atmosphere. IWS takes great pride in its excellent infrastructure, which has been suitably planned to accommodate learning and all-round development of the child. The school provides a full range of facilities to help the children adjust happily and feel at home as they learn and grow mentally, physically, and socially.

Our Vision

At Sai Siddhartha high school we are committed to provide education in a position safe and a stimulating environment for children to learn. Each and every child entrusted to our care, is treasured, nurtured, guided constantly and helped to become a holistic human being – Emotionally stable, intellectually vibrant, spiritually enlightened and socially committed which makes him build confidence and greater character.

Our Mission

To instill self-discipline in students, make them empathetic towards their fellow-mates and surroundings, improve academic performance through peer-teaching, make optimal use of audio-visual resources to make learning an interesting and everlasting experience.

Sai Siddhartha High School

Sai Siddhartha high school is a highly Progressive, Proactive and Vibrant Institution committed deeply to the cause of education. It has been doing a lot of research on the changing environment, emerging trends and understands the needs of the new generation. It has been successful in designing the curriculum to suit its needs by making it relevant to the present times, appealing and interesting. The school has broken through the confines of convention and has always been searching for breakthroughs to excel in the field of education through innovations.

The school has a deep desire to make its students emotionally and academically strong, well groomed and above all excellent, selfless, noble human beings who would make a difference in this world.

Our Dynamic Faculty

The school has the unique distinction of having an excellent team of teacher educators who are dynamic, creative, and innovative and have a passion for teaching. They are kind, warm and gentle and create a pleasant positive atmosphere of faith, trust and respect in the classroom.

The teachers are well versed with the latest trends in teaching methodologies and techniques. They are trained and equipped with the skills required for blending technology and traditional teaching methods seamlessly. It is mandatory for the teachers to undergo refresher courses, teacher enrichment programmers regularly and update themselves on the emerging trends and the best teaching practices to be ahead of times.
