Student Life

Life at
Sai Siddhartha High School

At Sai Siddhartha High School, We recognize the importance to prepare students for an increasingly complex and inter-connected world, we provide them with new ways of thinking and new ways of collaborating in solving the most pressing global issues.


We make learning relevant and exciting for students, as we continually draw connections between what they learn in the classroom and the real world. Perhaps most importantly, we encourage our young people to reflect on their learning and to act on it in the service of others. They are taught that they need the right attitude and discipline to achieve success. We believe that learning should inform action and create impact.

At sai Siddhartha School, We are digital citizens

We endeavor to use technology as a means of empowering students to think and act responsibly and ethically in the ever-evolving digital landscape. Digital citizenship, therefore, is taught across the curriculum. We integrate technology into classroom activities and the curriculum in order to provide rich and meaningful 21st-century learning experiences. A variety of devices, software applications, and web-based resources are used in effective, age-appropriate ways for students throughout each level of their learning. We are constantly investing in the infrastructure and a galaxy of online resources for knowledge and technology updates.

At sai Siddhartha School, We are social stewards

We encourage our students to respect differences and care for others, fostering values of responsibility and effort. Our community outreach program supports students in finding their interests and encourages involvement in making the world a better place. IWS Service in Action projects exemplify these values through dedicated community engagement. IWS Service in Action projects include:

  • Projects to encourage environmental stewardship.
  • Charity programs
  • Hosting special events to raise funds to help the unprivileged masses.
  • Supporting animal shelters and elderly homes

At Sai Siddhartha school, We are holistic learners

at School, we practice holistic education, blending curricular and co-curricular activities for students from Nursery to Grade 12. Our curriculum includes arts, drama, music, athletics, and other creative pursuits, fostering imaginative and productive engagement. Students participate in musicals, plays, festivals, debates, and art showcases throughout the school year. These activities promote cultural exploration, art as communication, and skills in performance, production, and event management. Our fully equipped auditorium and music rooms support our commitment to excellence in teaching and comprehensive evaluation.
