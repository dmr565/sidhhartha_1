Sai Siddhartha High School is affiliated to the Central Board of Secondary Education (ICSE) a leading Educational Board in the country.

The ICSE curriculum envisions a vibrant, robust and holistic school education. ICSE is committed to serving the children, through high quality educational endeavors, empowering them to contribute towards a humane, just and pluralistic society, promoting introspective living, by creating exciting learning opportunities, with a commitment to excellence.

Sai Siddhartha High School curriculum is child centered and encourages an integrated approach to provide intellectual, social and cultural vivacity among the learners. It emphasizes on creative, collaborative, conceptual, comprehensive, critical thinking and also development of life skills. This approach aims to develop compassionate and productive human beings.

ICSE curriculum prepares the child for higher studies and competitive exams. It also allows the students to opt for subjects of his/her own interests. The board’s standard gives the student an edge over their counterparts and achieve excellence in learning.

Our curriculum aims to ensure the students experience, coherence in their learning, which empowers them to achieve their potential and become independent individuals in the emerging society.
